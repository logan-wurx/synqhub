## New in beta 6

- **OnBoard Hub is now SYNQ.** New name, logo and app icon ("Every device. One system."). Installing it removes the old
  OnBoard Hub install first and keeps your settings, your EQ engine and SYNQ Helper's sign-in choice.
- **New look.** The room light behind every page takes the colours you last set on your lights, a floating dock replaces
  the sidebar, the Gear page opens with big live numbers and a bento grid, and the mouse page shows its pointer speed in
  a dial. The default accent follows your lights too.
- **Gear Doctor measures polling right.** Windows 11 slows the mouse reports a background listener gets to about 125 a
  second, so a 4 kHz mouse read "125 Hz". The checkup now listens through the app's own window.
- **The website ([synqhub.app](https://synqhub.app)) does nearly everything the app does.** In Chrome or Edge, with
  nothing installed: mice, keyboards and controllers, Govee lamps over Bluetooth, USB RGB hubs and Dynamic Lighting
  devices. With **SYNQ Helper** (one small download that installs itself, no administrator, and waits in the tray):
  monitors, webcams, Wi-Fi lights, screen sync across Wi-Fi lights and hubs, the EQ, and Razer devices on Windows. The
  website's checks are the app's own, in the browser or in the helper.
- **SYNQ Helper on its own.** `synq-helper.exe` next to the installer: run it once and it installs for you and starts
  with Windows. With the SYNQ app installed it turns that app's helper on instead.
